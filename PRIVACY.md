# Privacy Policy for Advanced Clipboard Manager

*Last updated: October 2026*

Advanced Clipboard Manager ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our software.

## 1. Information Collection and Storage
- **Local Data Only**: All clipboard history, copied text, snippets, images, and user preferences are processed and stored exclusively on your local device.
- **No Data Collection or Transmission**: The application does not collect, track, transmit, upload, or sync your clipboard data or personal information to any remote servers, cloud databases, or third-party services.
- **No Telemetry / Analytics**: We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) or background analytics telemetry.

## 2. Capabilities and Permissions
The application requests Windows system capabilities strictly to provide its core clipboard management functionality:
- **Clipboard Access**: Required to detect copied content and manage clipboard history locally.
- **Local Storage Access**: Required to store user configuration and local history database on your machine.

## 3. Data Control and Deletion
You maintain complete control over your data:
- You can search, edit, or delete individual clipboard items at any time.
- You can clear your entire history and database with one click in the application settings.
- Uninstalling the application removes all local data created by it.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The application does not integrate with third-party tracking, advertising, or data-sharing services.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this page.
